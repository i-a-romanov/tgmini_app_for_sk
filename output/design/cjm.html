User goal
Find a credible route to a Skolkovo grant.
Understand the value before sharing any information.
Get a plan that fits their goal and available time.
Make tangible progress and understand a strength.
Trust the plan enough to continue preparing.
Keep a manageable rhythm and meet verified deadlines.
Help another applicant without exposing personal information.
Actions
Sees a student-channel post; asks a friend; follows the bot link.
Opens the Mini App; reads the free-access and independence statement.
Chooses a goal and daily pace; optionally enables reminders; reviews plan.
Completes a short evidence-based exercise; connects action to outcome.
Returns to the plan after the first completed task; sees saved progress.
Returns for the next task; adjusts pace; uses optional bot reminders.
Shares a public app link after a useful milestone; optionally gives feedback.
Touchpoints
Student channels, peer recommendations, campaign deep link.
Bot profile, menu button, entry screen and Telegram header.
Three-step flow; native MainButton and BackButton; permission dialog.
First task, answer feedback, progress checklist, preparation material.
Free plan confirmation, task success state, home progress card.
Home, profile, bot messages, verified dates; calendar export later.
Profile share action, Telegram share sheet, peer conversation.

Pain points
Scattered information. “Is this for someone like me?”
Fear of spam or an unofficial service posing as admissions.
Too many questions; uncertainty about deadlines; notification fatigue.
Blank-page anxiety. Too much preparation content at once.
Mistaking activation for a submitted application or admission assurance.
Missed dates; stale guidance; notification overload; Telegram unavailable.
Unwanted exposure of progress or résumé; pressure to invite friends.
Opportunities
One clear promise; a useful sample task; student-made disclosure.
No phone request. Plain privacy explanation. One “Create my plan” action.
Short flow; saved draft; editable choices; reminders off by default.
One five-minute task; example answer; retry without penalty.
State exactly what is saved. Separate checklist progress from admission odds.
Source + verification date on deadlines; pause reminders; printable checklist.
Opt-in sharing of a neutral link; no private data; no referral gate.
